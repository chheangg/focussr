# Code of Conduct

Focussr is built by people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone should feel
welcome here.

**Please** be kind, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and help newcomers.

**Don't** harass, insult or demean anyone, post someone's private information, or keep contacting
someone who asked you to stop.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block anyone who breaks these rules.

To report a problem privately, use the
[private reporting form](https://github.com/chheangg/focussr/security/advisories/new) and start the title
with "Conduct". Reports are kept confidential.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
